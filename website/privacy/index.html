Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

1. Who we are

Lofer is an early-stage, unincorporated project based in the United Kingdom, developing intelligent, personalised body-care technology.

Lofer is jointly operated by Zuyi Gong and Leyi Jiang, who act as joint data controllers for the personal information described in this policy.

For privacy-related questions or requests, you can contact us at:

loferprivacy@gmail.com

Both founders are responsible for ensuring that personal information is handled appropriately.

The email address above serves as our central contact point for privacy requests.

2. Information we collect

When you join our waitlist, we collect the following:

Name and email address

We use this information to register you on the waitlist and contact you about early-access opportunities.

Pricing preferences (optional)

We may ask how much you would be willing to pay for a Lofer package containing six intelligent body-care patches.

Your response helps us understand customer preferences and inform future product development and pricing.

Marketing preferences (optional)

We record whether you have chosen to receive additional product news, updates and promotional communications from Lofer.

We may also collect administrative information, including the date of registration, marketing consent timestamps and the version of the consent wording presented to you.

Website information

When you visit our website, our hosting and security providers may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser information and request logs to operate and protect the website.

We do not intentionally collect medical records, diagnoses or detailed health information through our waitlist.

3. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • Manage waitlist registrations.
  • Contact you about your registration and early-access availability.
  • Understand potential customers' pricing preferences.
  • Inform Lofer's product development and launch planning.
  • Send optional marketing communications where you have expressly opted in.
  • Maintain website functionality and security.
  • Respond to privacy-related requests.

We do not sell your personal information or use waitlist responses to make medical assessments.

4. Our legal basis for processing

Under UK GDPR, we rely on the following lawful bases:

Legitimate interests

We process your name, email address and optional pricing preferences to operate our waitlist, respond to your requests and understand interest in Lofer.

We consider these interests alongside your rights and reasonable expectations.

Consent

We rely on your consent to send optional marketing communications.

Marketing consent is voluntary and is not required to join the waitlist.

You may withdraw your consent at any time without affecting your waitlist registration.

Where other applicable privacy laws require additional consent or safeguards, we will implement these before undertaking the relevant processing.

5. Marketing communications and unsubscribing

Joining our waitlist does not automatically subscribe you to marketing emails.

We will only send you additional product news, promotional updates and offers if you have explicitly opted in.

Every marketing email will include an unsubscribe link.

You can unsubscribe at any time without creating an account or providing a reason.

Unsubscribing from marketing does not remove you from the waitlist.

If you would like to leave the waitlist entirely or request deletion of your information, contact us at:

loferprivacy@gmail.com

We may retain a minimal record of your marketing opt-out where necessary to ensure your preferences are respected.

6. How we store and protect your information

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information.

Waitlist information is stored using Supabase, a cloud database service.

Access to personal information is restricted to authorised individuals who require it for legitimate operational purposes.

We take reasonable measures to protect information against unauthorised access, misuse, loss or disclosure.

We do not make waitlist information publicly accessible.

7. Sharing your information

We may share personal information with trusted service providers that help us operate Lofer.

These may include:

  • Supabase: Waitlist database storage.
  • GitHub Pages (provided by GitHub): Hosting and delivering our website.
  • Email service providers: Sending registration, early-access and optional marketing emails, if implemented.

We do not share personal information with third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

Where required, we use appropriate contractual arrangements with service providers to protect personal information.

8. International visitors and data transfers

Lofer is based in the United Kingdom, but our website may be accessed by people in other countries.

If you join our waitlist from outside the UK, your personal information may be processed in the UK or in countries where our service providers operate.

Where required by applicable data protection laws, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers, including recognised adequacy arrangements or approved contractual protections.

Depending on your location, you may have additional privacy rights under local laws.

For questions about international data processing, contact us at:

loferprivacy@gmail.com

9. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

Our intended retention approach is to:

  • Keep active registrations while preparing for Lofer's initial launch.
  • Periodically review waitlist records.
  • Review remaining waitlist information within 12 months after the initial product launch and delete or anonymise information no longer required.
  • Remove users from marketing lists when they unsubscribe, while retaining minimal opt-out records where necessary.

You may request deletion of your personal information at any time, subject to applicable legal requirements.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Withdraw consent to marketing.
  • Request a copy of certain information in a portable format.
  • Lodge a complaint with an applicable data protection authority.

To exercise your rights, contact:

loferprivacy@gmail.com

If you are based in the United Kingdom, you may also contact the Information Commissioner's Office at:

https://ico.org.uk

11. Cookies and website technologies

Our website may use technologies necessary for its operation, security and performance.

If we introduce non-essential cookies, analytics or advertising technologies, we will provide appropriate information and obtain consent where required.

12. Children's privacy

Our waitlist is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the waitlist.

If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us at:

loferprivacy@gmail.com

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Lofer develops.

When we make changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Where required, we will provide additional notice or obtain consent before introducing materially different uses of personal information.
