Our mission

Everyday aches shouldn't leave you guessing.

A physiotherapist can tell you where a problem is likely coming from and how to ease it, but that takes an appointment, time and money. Home wellness devices like massage guns and heat pads are always to hand, but they leave every decision to you: where to apply it, which setting to use, for how long, and when to stop.

Lofer brings together the best of both. It listens to how you describe your symptoms, asks a few follow-up questions, and suggests which area of your body needs the most care and which session to run. Then it delivers the session itself, with built-in safety controls, and asks how it went. When something sounds like it needs a professional, it says so.

The best of both, at home.

The Lofer App

It starts with how you feel.

You describe what you feel. Lofer asks follow-up questions that refine what happens next, and guides a simple movement check before suggesting a session. Some answers lead to a session. Others suggest you pause and speak to a professional.

The Lofer Loop

Six steps, one connected process.

Each step feeds the next, so a session begins with information, and what happens afterwards counts.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Capture what you're feeling and the context around it.

  2. 02

    Consider

    Work out what still needs clarifying.

  3. 03

    Check

    Ask or test only what changes the next decision.

  4. 04

    Act

    Deliver the selected care, when care is the right next step.

  5. 05

    Reassess

    Compare how it feels, and the same movement, before and after.

  6. 06

    Learn

    Keep the record, so it informs the next decision.

The Lofer tile

One small tile, four functions.

  • Heat

    A 20 × 30 mm heater film, with a thermal cut-off in the chassis.

  • Vibration massage

    A vibration motor in the rigid chassis, from a soft hum to a deeper pulse.

  • Micro-current

    Gentle micro-current stimulation, planned for Lofer. Not yet part of the current hardware or tile design.

  • Muscle signals

    Two EMG electrodes built into the hydrogel liner, designed to read muscle activity. Not yet validated.

  • Soft to the touch

    A silicone cover wraps the top and sides, with a stitched seam and the logo debossed into it. Shown in five colourways: clay, sand, oat, sage and cocoa.

  • A gentle skin side

    A hydrogel liner sits against your skin, with two built-in EMG electrodes. The heater film sits behind it.

  • Latches edge to edge

    Edge magnets pull tiles together. Each tile has 18 gold contacts, three on each of its six edges, designed so tiles can link up.

  • One cable charges four

    The dock has spring contacts in every slot, and any edge of a tile fits any slot. A warm amber light shows while it charges, and goes off when full.

Personal

Your next session starts with a history.

Lofer keeps a record of what you reported, what was checked, which session you ran and how it felt afterwards. The next time something aches, it starts from that record, not from scratch. You can also share a summary with a professional.
