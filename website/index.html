Our mission

Everyday aches shouldn't leave you guessing.

A physiotherapist can tell you where a problem is likely coming from and how to work on it, but that takes an appointment, time and money. Home tools like massage guns and heat pads are always to hand, but they leave every decision to you: where to work, which setting, how long, and when to stop.

Lofer is designed to bridge that gap. It listens to how you describe your symptoms, asks the follow-up questions that matter, and guides you to the most plausible area to focus on, and how to work on it. Then it delivers the care itself, within safety limits, and checks how it went. When something sounds like it needs a professional, it says so.

The best of both, at home.

Guided checks

Where it hurts is where we start.

You describe what you feel. Lofer asks the follow-up questions that change what happens next, and guides a simple movement check before suggesting a session. Some answers lead to a session. Others lead to stopping, and getting it looked at.

The care loop

Six steps, one connected process.

Each step feeds the next, so a session begins with information, and what happens afterwards counts.

  1. 01

    Listen

    Capture what you're feeling and the context around it.

  2. 02

    Consider

    Work out what still needs clarifying.

  3. 03

    Check

    Ask or test only what changes the next decision.

  4. 04

    Act

    Deliver the selected care, when care is the right next step.

  5. 05

    Reassess

    Compare how it feels, and the same movement, before and after.

  6. 06

    Learn

    Keep the record, so it informs the next decision.

Personal

Your next session starts with a history.

Lofer keeps a record of what you reported, what was checked, what you tried and how it felt afterwards. The next time something aches, it starts from that record, not from scratch. You can also share a summary with a professional.

Inside Lofer

Built to sense and act.

Each Lofer tile layers warmth, vibration and sensing into a 10.8 mm body. Scroll to take one apart. These are our prototype designs; details will change as we test.

  1. 01

    Lid

    A soft-touch top shell in the Lofer shape.

  2. 02

    Core

    Two 2.5 mm battery strips, two electronics islands, a 15 × 15 mm Peltier island and a coin vibration motor.

  3. 03

    Frame

    Magnetic edge contacts, three per edge (N · TX, GND, S · RX), designed so tiles can link edge to edge.

  4. 04

    Skin side

    A 20 × 30 mm heater, two Ø8 mm EMG electrodes, and a temperature sensor with thermal cut-off.

  • Warmth

    A film heater and a Peltier element, watched by a temperature sensor with a thermal cut-off.

  • Vibration

    A coin vibration motor, from a soft hum to a deeper pulse.

  • Muscle signals

    Two skin-side EMG electrodes, designed to read muscle activity. Not yet validated.

  • Modular tiles

    Magnetic edge contacts, and a dock that charges the tiles together.

  • Intensity limits set per area and situation, applied before anything starts.
  • Pause or stop at any moment, by tap or by voice.
  • Feedback during the session: say it's too strong, and it eases off. Say it's worse, and it pauses.
